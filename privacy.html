Privacy

This page explains in plain language what QuizMaster stores and why. It is a summary of how the app works, not a legal document.

What is stored online

  • A sign-in identifier. Guest players get a random ID. Players who use Google sign-in get an ID linked to their Google account. QuizMaster does not read or store your email address, real name or photo.
  • Your nickname and your stats: games played and best score.
  • Leaderboard scores: your nickname, points, difficulty level, topic and the time the game was finished.
  • Questions you create, together with your sign-in ID, so that only you can delete them.

Who can see it

  • The leaderboard shows nickname, points, level and topic to signed-in players.
  • Questions you create can be played by everyone.
  • Your sign-in ID and stats are not shown to other players.

Please use a nickname

Choose a nickname that does not include your real name, school, address or contact details.

What is kept on your device

  • Your light or dark theme choice and a copy of your best score.
  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in.
  • A saved copy of the app, so it can open without internet.

QuizMaster does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and it does not use analytics.

Services that process data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) stores the online data listed above.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser requests the font files from Google when a page loads, which shares your IP address with Google.

Removing your data
